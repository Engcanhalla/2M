문제1주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것은?4점
Years ago, there lived a boy in a small village in Scotland.
(A) One cold winter morning, Bruce and the other sparrows were in a tree. The tree was covered with snow. Angus saw Bruce raising his feet one by one. "Your feet are cold! Maybe I can help you," said Angus. (B) His name was Angus. A sparrow, Bruce, was his close friend. When they played together, Bruce flew into Angus's pocket. (C) Angus's father owned a small store that made socks, and Angus sometimes helped him. The store used to have many customers, but recently it lost a lot of them. Villagers started buying socks from a big department store.
정답 ▸ 4번 · 먼저 앵거스와 브루스의 우정을 소개하는 (B)가 이어지고, 이어서 아버지 가게의 상황을 설명하는 (C)가 오며, 마지막으로 추운 겨울 아침 장면인 (A)가 이어지는 것이 자연스럽다. 따라서 (B) - (C) - (A)가 정답이다.
문제2주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것은?4점
One cold winter morning, Bruce and the other sparrows were in a tree.
(A) After the store closed for the night, Angus sat in front of the sock machine. Soon, a tiny pair of socks popped out. Warm and soft winter socks with red stripes! (B) Angus made more and more socks because he knew other sparrows were shaking in the cold weather, too. But he didn't realize that he used up all the wool in the machine. (C) The tree was covered with snow. Angus saw Bruce raising his feet one by one. "Your feet are cold! Maybe I can help you," said Angus.
정답 ▸ 3번 · 나무에 있던 브루스의 모습을 묘사하는 (C)가 먼저 오고, 앵거스가 양말을 만드는 (A)가 이어진 뒤, 다른 참새들을 위해 계속 양말을 만든다는 (B)로 마무리되는 것이 자연스럽다. 따라서 (C) - (A) - (B)가 정답이다.

② 패러프레이징(para) — 아래 지문을 참고하여 푸시오
Years ago, there lived a boy in a small village in Scotland. His name was Angus. A sparrow, Bruce, was his close friend. When they played together, Bruce flew into Angus's pocket. Angus's father owned a small store that made socks, and Angus sometimes helped him. The store used to have many customers, but recently it lost a lot of them. Villagers started buying socks from a big department store. One cold winter morning, Bruce and the other sparrows were in a tree. The tree was covered with snow. Angus saw Bruce raising his feet one by one. "Your feet are cold! Maybe I can help you," said Angus. After the store closed for the night, Angus sat in front of the sock machine. Soon, a tiny pair of socks popped out. Warm and soft winter socks with red stripes! Angus made more and more socks because he knew other sparrows were shaking in the cold weather, too. But he didn't realize that he used up all the wool in the machine.
