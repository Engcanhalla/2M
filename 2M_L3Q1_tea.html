1. 다음 글에 드러난 'G'의 심경 변화로 가장 적절한 것은?

G: I saw that your drone passed through the rings. That was cool!
M: Thanks. Do you want to try it?
G: I'd love to. How can I fly the drone?
M: You can use this controller.
G: Could you explain more about it?
M: Press the green button, and the drone goes up.
G: I see. What's next?
M: Press the red button, and it goes down.
G: It works! Thank you so much.

①  curious → excited

②  bored → interested

③  nervous → relieved

④  worried → disappointed

⑤  surprised → calm

✔ 정답: 1번

G는 드론이 링을 통과하는 것을 보고 'That was cool!'이라며 관심과 호기심을 드러내고, 직접 조종하는 방법을 묻는다. 이후 버튼을 눌러 드론을 직접 조종하는 데 성공하자 'It works! Thank you so much.'라며 기쁨과 흥분을 표현한다. 따라서 G의 심경은 '호기심 있는(curious) → 신이 난(excited)'으로 변화하는 것이 가장 적절하다. ②는 처음부터 지루해하는 상황이 아니므로 오답이며, ③은 긴장과 안도의 흐름으로 본문의 분위기와 맞지 않는다.

2. 다음 글에 드러난 'Juwon'의 심경 변화로 가장 적절한 것은?

①  nervous → relieved

②  proud → embarrassed

③  proud → confident

④  scared → excited

⑤  surprised → disappointed

✔ 정답: 3번

Juwon은 처음에 수상 소감을 말하며 'I'm proud of my invention.'이라고 자신의 발명품에 대한 자부심(proud)을 드러냅니다. 이후 발명품의 원리를 설명하고 마지막에 'I'm sure that this invention will be very helpful.'이라며 발명품의 유용성에 대한 강한 확신(confident)을 나타냅니다. 따라서 심경 변화는 'proud → confident'가 가장 적절합니다. ①번 'nervous → relieved'는 글에 긴장이나 안도의 표현이 없으므로 오답이며, ②번 'proud → embarrassed'는 후반부에 부끄러움의 표현이 없으므로 오답입니다.

6. 밑줄 친 came by chance가 다음 글에서 의미하는 바로 가장 적절한 것은?

A New Cookie Star: Chocolate Chip Cookies
In America, there was a famous cook, Ms. Wakefield. One day in 1938, she wanted to bake chocolate cookies. However, she didn't have enough time to melt chocolate. So, she cut a chocolate bar into small chips. Then, she mixed them with her dough and put the cookie dough into the oven. When Ms. Wakefield opened the oven, she found that the chips were not melted into the dough. Although the chips remained in the cookies, she was not disappointed. Her new cookies were so delicious. She invented chocolate CHIP cookies!

Finding Safety in Glass: Safety Glass
In 1903, a French scientist, Mr. Bénédictus, was experimenting with liquid plastic in a glass flask. He accidentally hit the glass flask, and it fell on the floor. Although the glass flask was broken into pieces, they still held together in the shape of the flask! How could this happen? The secret was the liquid plastic in the flask. This finding led to his invention of safety glass. These days, we can find safety glass everywhere. For example, it is used for car windows. If there is a car accident, people are safer from sharp pieces of glass.

Cleaning Clothes Without Water: Dry Cleaning
Can you clean your clothes without water? The answer is "yes." Dry cleaning uses oil to clean clothes. There's an interesting story about dry cleaning. In the 1800s, a factory worker in France spilled lamp oil on a tablecloth. The factory manager, Mr. Jolly, looked at the tablecloth and found something. The dirty spot on the tablecloth was cleaned by the lamp oil! His discovery led to a new way of cleaning cleaning clothes. Now, dry cleaning is widely used around the world. These wonderful inventions came by chance. When things go wrong, don't be disappointed. Maybe you can be the next amazing inventor.
